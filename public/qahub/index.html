Welcome to HUFS Center for Open Research in Esports: QA HUB

Student Game QA & Feedback Hub.

This page is a mini-hub for experiencing and participating in a QA (testing) conference on games developed by students participating in the "Esports Game Design and Production" program at the Department of Global Sports Industry at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies. After playing, please wait for a moment to use the feedback link on the game screen. This will greatly assist in improving the game.

How to use

Select a semester, play a project build, and submit feedback using the form link on each card. Your QA comments support iterative improvement and help students document postmortems with evidence.

Featured Projects

Browse student projects by semester. Thumbnails and project links are loaded from the course sheet.
